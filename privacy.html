Privacy Policy

Locus Magnus · Effective October 27, 2026

Locus Magnus is a simple app that shows one mantra or quote at a time. It is developed and maintained by Emilien Lecoffre, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Locus Magnus does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Locus Magnus has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information on your own device only, so it can work properly:

  • Your light or dark theme preference, if you choose one.
  • A copy of the app and of the mantras and quotes, so it can open quickly and work offline.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Hosting and network requests

The app and its content are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When the app loads or refreshes its content, your device connects to GitHub's servers. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the content and keep the service secure. We do not have access to this information. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

On Android, the app opens its content using Google Chrome or your default browser engine, which is governed by that browser's own privacy policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personal data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children

Locus Magnus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app adds features that handle personal data, such as notifications, this page will describe them before they are released.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy, you can reach the developer through www.emilien-lecoffre.com.
